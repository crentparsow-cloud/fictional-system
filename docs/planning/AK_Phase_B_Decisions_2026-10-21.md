# Phase B decisions, 21 October 2026

Suggested by Claude on Crent's instruction. Every value here is a proposal and sits on the pre-launch review list. All prices are set in config or the database, never hard-coded, so each can change without a code release.

## Confirmed by Crent

- Streak rules approved: opt-in, offered after a few finished steps, counts finished steps, automatic free pause, current and longest shown quietly, no leaderboards, never on mental health shelves (tier 1 and 2).
- Companion concept approved: grows from finished steps, returns with a line or question, never loses anything.
- No theological reviewer for now. Workbooks are made from existing books. Revisit before any faith-specific original content.

## Organisation prices and terms (proposed)

- Small-organisation plan: 5 to 50 seats, annual only, £60 a seat a year (the same as £5 a month). Seats can be added any time, prorated. Seats can only be reduced at renewal. Members join through one unique sign-up link.
- Church plans by average Sunday attendance, annual, unlimited members within the band:
  - Under 50: £120 a year.
  - 50 to 149: £240 a year.
  - 150 to 399: £480 a year.
  - 400 and over: £900 a year.
- Six-seat group licence: one workbook for six people, £39 one-off, no renewal. Matches how UK churches and book groups buy study material.
- Larger buyers (over 50 seats): a 30-day pilot switched on by Akana staff, then an annual invoice.
- Small teams: a 14-day refund window instead of a pilot.
- Prices are published on the Organisations page.
- Group leader kit is included with every organisation plan and the six-seat licence.

## Referrals (proposed)

- Double-sided. The friend gets 14 extra trial days on top of the normal trial (28 days monthly, 35 days annual). A friend on a two-person plan, which has no trial, gets £5 off the first payment instead.
- The referrer gets a £5 account credit when the friend's first payment clears. A referrer still on trial gets 14 extra trial days instead of credit.
- Cap of 12 rewarded referrals a year for each reader. Credit never turns into cash.

## Affiliate share (proposed)

- Authors: 25 percent of membership revenue from members they refer, paid for 12 months from the member's first payment.
- Book affiliate links (to the source book): use the retailer's own programme. Akana keeps the commission and shows a plain disclosure line.

## "Send this workbook" (proposed)

- One gift each quarter for each paying member. It does not roll over.
- The recipient must never have paid Akana.
- The author is paid 50 percent of the workbook's list price from the marketing pool.

## Other values chosen during the build

- Gift memberships: 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at the matching monthly price multiplied out, using the annual price for 12 months. They never renew.
- Student price: 50 percent off membership, checked by an academic email address, re-checked each year.
- Pause membership: 1, 2 or 3 months. It is the only save offer.
- Win-back emails: to lapsed monthly members and one-off buyers only, at most one each season. None to lapsed annual members.
- Social proof: only shown when a number is real and above 100.
