# Catalogue fill: Personal Growth, Love and Relationships, Family and Parenting

Group `growth-love-family`. Prepared 2026-10-07. Machine-readable twin: `growth-love-family.json` in this folder.

## Summary

All 12 subcategories now hold at least three workbooks. This adds 25 workbooks: 7 public-domain classics and 18 demo titles. It adds 12 authors: 3 real public-domain authors and 9 invented demo authors. Six existing authors are reused: Benjamin Franklin and Arnold Bennett (real, demo catalogue), Henry David Thoreau (AU-F4SW6) and Cicero (AU-48E8W) (real, already minted by the mind-health group for different works), Clodagh Ní Fhaoláin-Burke and Sofia Hedlund-Ahl (demo). No new imprints are proposed.

Four subcategories were already full from the demo catalogue: Chosen Habits, Partners in Step, Companionable Days and Raising With Care. Chosen Habits and Companionable Days each gain one classic anyway, because Franklin's virtues and Cicero's friendship dialogue fit so closely.

All codes are provisional. They were minted with the catalogue code_rule, keys `workbook:<id>` and `author:<slug>`, and checked against every code in `content/` and `docs/`.

**Title checks are pending.** The shared web search limit for this session ran out after the author name checks. None of the 18 demo titles has had its exact-phrase search. Each is marked pending in the JSON and must be searched before use. The classics need no title check.

## Public-domain reasoning

Each classic meets Research 4's Tier A test: published before 1931, and every author, translator and editor died before 1946. That clears the UK and Ireland (life plus 70) and the US (published before 1931). Canada, Australia and New Zealand are still to confirm by the Legal Lead, as for the existing classics. Editions were checked on the Project Gutenberg catalogue pages on 7 October 2026. Each title still needs a house record in `docs/public-domain/` and a signed review. Quote only from the Gutenberg transcription, strip the Gutenberg header and licence, and do not use the Gutenberg name in marketing.

## Left out

- **James Allen, As a Man Thinketh (1903).** Public domain (Allen died 1912), but it makes outcome claims: the chapter 'Effect of Thought on Health and the Body' ties illness to thought, and the book ties circumstances and poverty to thinking. Fails the no health or wealth claims rule.
- **Seneca, tr. Richard M. Gummere, Moral Letters to Lucilius (Loeb, 1917 to 1925).** Gummere died 1969, so the translation is in UK copyright until the end of 2039. Use Aubrey Stewart's 1889 translation of the dialogues instead.
- **Benjamin Franklin, ed. Frank Woodworth Pine, Autobiography, Project Gutenberg #20203.** The editor's death year is not recorded, so the editorial matter cannot be cleared for the UK. Use the Eliot text, #148.
- **Wallace D. Wattles, The Science of Getting Rich and others.** Money and health claims.
- **Dale Carnegie, How to Win Friends and Influence People.** Not public domain (Carnegie died 1955 and the 1936 book is in copyright in the US; later revised editions too).
- **Florence Nightingale, Notes on Nursing (1859).** Public domain and written for people nursing relatives at home, but it is period medical advice. Considered for Caring Hands and left out on safety grounds.
- **L. Emmett Holt, The Care and Feeding of Children (1894).** Public domain but outdated infant feeding and care advice. Left out of Early Days on safety grounds.

## Safety notes

- New Chapters is standard tier and not for groups. All three titles carry Help now, the when-home-is-not-safe signpost and, where separation is involved, the legal advice note. Abuse itself is not a topic for any title in this set. It belongs to a higher-tier design and is not group suitable.
- Early Days titles carry Help now, `not_medical_advice` and a new `new_parent_support_lines` signpost. They give no feeding, sleep or development advice, and send health questions to a midwife, health visitor or doctor.
- Changing Families titles carry Help now and the family support signpost. Kinship care often follows hard events, so the workbook does not ask about the reasons.
- Caring Hands titles carry Help now and a new `carer_support_lines` signpost. No dementia or health advice. Benefits, power of attorney and care funding are named and pointed to official services only.
- No title in this set makes a therapy, health, wealth or outcome claim. Relationship and parenting titles describe practical steps only.
- `safety_profile: standard` is a new value for non-wellbeing titles on standard-tier Themes. The catalogue so far only uses `none` and `wellbeing_standard` [check with the Clinical Safety Lead].

## Name and title risks

- All 18 demo titles: exact-phrase searches not yet run. 'A Polite No', 'The Small Legacy' and 'Making Room for Everyone' are short, common phrases and are the most likely to match something.
- Rukhsana Begum-Thornley: close in sound to the boxer and author Ruqsana Begum. Spelling and surname differ. Legal may prefer a change.
- Jamal Whitacre-Odom: shares a surname with the basketball player Lamar Odom. Low risk.
- 'Of the Shortness of Life' chapter numbers should be confirmed against the Stewart text.
- New area `direction-and-meaning` and new Themes Quiet Confidence, Clearer Choices, Living With Purpose, Wisdom for Living, New Chapters, Early Days, Changing Families and Caring Hands still need their own clearance, as categories.md says.
