# Catalogue fill: Mind and Mood, Health and Body

Group `mind-health`. Prepared 7 October 2026. The machine-readable version is `mind-health.json` in this folder. Every new AK- and AU- code is provisional.

## Summary

All 11 subcategories now hold at least 3 workbooks. 13 workbooks are new: 10 invented demo titles and 3 public-domain classics. 8 authors are new: 5 invented and 3 real public-domain authors. Two existing invented authors, Haruka Tanabe-Ellis and Nadia Farouk-Hassan, take one new title each. No new imprint was needed. Two new authors sit with Kettlebrook Editions and three are independent.

Programme lengths across the new titles: four of 4 weeks, four of 6 weeks, three of 8 weeks and two of 12 weeks.

Maya Vaughn's 20 titles count where their theme fits. Eighteen are placed. Two are not counted here: Finding Your People (loneliness) and Wired Differently (focus), because no subcategory in these two categories fits them.

## Safety notes

- Every new title is genre wellbeing, so every one carries Help now and the full claims check, and none has a scored self-check.
- One Window Open is the only higher-tier new title. It sits in Brighter Days, which the taxonomy marks higher. It names no condition, makes no claim about depression and encourages professional help in week 10. The Clinical Safety Lead should sign off weeks 1 and 10.
- Tier mismatch to decide [Crent]: the taxonomy marks Settled Mind and Brighter Days as higher, but four Maya Vaughn titles placed there are standard in the seed (Quieting the Noise, Still Standing, Facing the Room and Finding Your Way Back). Re-tiering a live title gives `SAFETY_CHANGED` and a fresh sign-off.
- Health and Body titles make no medical, diet, weight or fitness claims. Movement titles tell readers with a health condition to check with a GP first. Ageing Well titles treat balance and memory as everyday habits, not falls prevention or memory treatment.
- The two food titles carry a proposed `eating_support_lines` signpost for readers who find food planning hard. That group does not exist in `support_lines.json` yet, so it needs adding and checking by market before seeding, or the field set to null [check].
- On Old Age discusses death in week 5. It stays standard because it is philosophical reflection, but the week can be skipped and the grief lines are one tap away.
- No advice note in the schema fits health titles. The claims check carries that load. A `not_medical_advice` note would be an additive schema change if wanted [Crent].
- Moving Through It and Nourished are counted under Health and Body by theme only. Their genre and tier do not change. If they are ever moved there as live titles, that is a safety-line change.
- Health and Body has no areas yet, so `area_id` is null on its titles [check].
