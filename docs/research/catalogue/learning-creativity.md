# Learning and Skills, Creativity and Making: new catalogue titles

Prepared 2026-10-07 by the learning-creativity catalogue agent. The machine-readable twin is `learning-creativity.json` in this folder. Nothing outside these two files was changed.

## Summary

This adds 19 workbooks: 7 on public-domain classics and 12 demo titles. It adds 5 real public-domain authors and 5 invented demo authors. It reuses Arnold Bennett, Matilda Kershaw-Nguyen, Olivier Tremblay-Singh and Rafael Moreira Lins from the existing catalogue. No new imprints are needed. Every subcategory in both categories now holds at least three workbooks.

All codes are provisional. They follow code_rule and were checked against every code in the repo on the day, but other groups were minting in parallel, so recheck at merge.

## Public-domain reasoning

The test is the one used in faith-titles.json. UK and Ireland: life plus 70 years from the end of the year the last author, translator or editor died. US: published before 1 January 1931. Every classic here was published before 1931, and every author and editor died by 1952. Each edition was checked on its Project Gutenberg catalogue page on 7 October 2026, which gave the life dates and the "public domain in the USA" status. Publication years not shown on those pages are marked [confirm]. Each title still needs a house record in docs/public-domain/ and a signed review. The Canadian, Australian and New Zealand terms are not recorded in the repo and are left to the Legal Lead.

John Dewey died in 1952. That is after the Research 4 Tier A cut-off of 1946, so How We Think is recorded as Tier B. The UK and Irish terms ended on 31 December 2022 and US status rests on the 1910 publication.

Dorothea Brande's Becoming a Writer was dropped. It is clear in the UK, because Brande died in 1948. It is not clear in the US. The 1961 renewals list from the Copyright Office has a cross-reference for Becoming a Writer under her son Justin Brande, pointing to her married name, Collins. That shows the copyright was renewed. A 1934 work renewed on time stays in copyright in the US until the end of 2029. The page read was truncated before the full Collins entry, so the renewal number is not recorded [check]. Bennett's The Author's Craft (1914) takes its place in Steady Writing.

## Risks and open checks

- Seven demo titles were not searched because the shared web search budget ran out: Twenty Minutes at the Table, The Short Story Season, Before the Names Are Lost, Letters to Open Later, Stories Told Out Loud, Something Made Each Week and The Walk With a Camera. They need an exact-phrase search and the ISBN check before any public use.
- One name was replaced. Tessa Moorcroft-Liang was dropped because Moorcroft is a well-known pottery maker. The five kept names returned no person of that name. A web search is not a full check.
- Kettlebrook Editions' imprint note covers career, relationship and wellbeing titles. Adding Aoife Brannigan-Oduya (education) and Ailsa Fenwick-Dhillon (life writing) widens it. Larchmere Atlas Books takes Marisol Abernathy-Kwan, which also widens its note.
- The Chemical History of a Candle must not ask readers to repeat Faraday's demonstrations.
- The Voyage of the Beagle carries period attitudes that the notes should name plainly.
- Walden and the Franklin autobiography were dropped because other groups already hold them (see the JSON `excluded` list). Both suit this group's Themes as cross-listings.
- Letters to Open Later, Stories Told Out Loud and Before the Names Are Lost may stir grief or difficult discoveries. They are personal development titles with no safety tier, but the Clinical Safety Lead should decide on a support signpost.
- Pause Before You Click needs current reporting routes for each market at build time.
- Chapter and letter groupings in the classic outlines were set from the contents as recalled and checked only where noted. Confirm them against each file before writing.
- The new area `writing-and-making` and the six new Themes follow docs/research/categories.md. None has had a register search.
