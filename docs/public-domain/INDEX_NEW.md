# Public-domain records: new classics (F-152)

House records for the 50 new public-domain classics in docs/research/catalogue/*.json (every workbooks_new entry with a pd_record block, excluding status removed_bible_study). Written 7 October 2026. None is signed. Codes are provisional.

33 are ready for the signer. 17 need a check first. "Ready for signer" means the edition is fixed and the UK, Irish and US conclusions rest on checked facts; the Canadian, Australian and New Zealand rows stay [to confirm] in every record, as in the F-118 records. "Needs check" gives the first open point.

Tier B titles (author died 1946 to 1955) are marked. Their UK, Irish and US terms have expired, but they need a launch decision on Tier B before signing: AK-DQXE1 (Sheldon, died 1946), AK-WXE4R (Strunk, died 1946) and AK-HHGT0 (Dewey, died 1952).

The JSON twins are staged in docs/public-domain/json/. They are not yet loaded by apps/web/lib/public-domain.ts, because the workbooks are not in the seeded catalogue and the codes are provisional. Move each twin to content/public-domain/ and add it to the RECORDS list when its workbook is seeded.
