# Interactive workbook licence

Version 0.1.0-draft. This is placeholder wording so the signing flow can be built and tested. It is not a contract. The lawyer replaces it with the approved text, which gets a new version number. Only an approved version can be signed for a real book.

## 1. The parties

This licence is between the rights holder named on the signing page ("you") and [company name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number] ("Akana").

## 2. What you grant

You grant Akana the right to make an interactive workbook from the book named on the signing page, and to sell it and make it available to readers on Akana and on white-label sites Akana runs, in the territories and for the term shown on the signing page.

The workbook uses short passages, ideas and structure from the book. It does not reproduce the book in full.

## 3. Variable terms

These are set per book on the signing page and recorded with your signature:

- Territory: worldwide, or a list of countries, with any countries left out.
- Term: how long the licence lasts, in months, from the date you sign.
- Exclusivity: whether Akana is the only interactive workbook for the book, and until when.
- Subscription: whether the workbook may be included in Akana membership.
- Cover: whether Akana may show the book's cover.
- Audio: whether Akana may make audio of the workbook text.

## 4. What buyers keep

A reader who buys the workbook keeps access to the version they bought and to their own answers, even if this licence ends. Akana stops selling the workbook to new readers when the licence ends.

## 5. Money

Revenue shares, the subscription pool share and any advance are set out in [schedule to be drafted]. Nothing on the signing page sets a rate.

## 6. Your promises

You confirm that:

- you hold the rights needed to grant this licence, or are authorised by the person who does;
- this licence does not clash with any publishing contract, KDP Select enrolment or other exclusive deal for the book;
- you will not make health, medical or guaranteed-result claims about the workbook in your own marketing.

## 7. Akana's promises

Akana will build and review the workbook, show you the final version before it goes live, label it accurately, and never use your manuscript or the book to train AI models.
