## 2. What we collect and why

**Account details.** Your email address, the name you give us, your region and your language. We use these to create and run your account, sign you in and show prices in your currency. Lawful basis: performing our contract with you.

**Your work in a workbook.** Your answers, check-ins, daily checks and plans. We store them so you can pick up where you left off on any device, and so you can export them. Lawful basis: performing our contract with you, and your explicit consent where the content is health data (see section 3).

**Progress.** Which units you have opened and which steps you have completed. We use this to show your progress, to work out what authors are paid from the membership pool, and to improve the service in aggregate. Lawful basis: performing our contract, and our legitimate interest in running the service and paying authors fairly. Author and publisher dashboards show only counts, and small counts are suppressed, so no author can identify you.

**Purchases and membership.** What you bought, when, how much you paid and the tax charged. Our payment processor handles your card details; we never see your full card number. Lawful basis: performing our contract, and our legal duty to keep tax and accounting records.

**Consents and acceptances.** Which version of our terms and which consents you agreed to, and when. Lawful basis: our legal duty to show that consent was given.

**Emails we send.** Receipts, sign-in links and account notices are sent without marketing consent because they are part of the service. Progress emails, reminders, suggestions and news are sent only if you turn them on. You can turn each one off in your account at any time. Lawful basis: performing our contract for service emails; your consent for the rest. Our emails never name the workbook you are using.

**Technical and security data.** Server logs with IP address, browser type and the pages requested, kept for a short time to keep the service secure and to find faults. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in security. Search queries are not stored with your account.

**Support.** What you send us when you contact support. Lawful basis: performing our contract and our legitimate interest in answering you.

We do not collect your location beyond the region you choose. We do not profile you for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not place advertising or analytics trackers on any page.

## 3. Special category data

Some workbooks are about wellbeing. What you write in them may reveal information about your mental or physical health. That is special category data under the UK GDPR.

Before you open your first wellbeing workbook we show you a short consent screen that explains this and asks for your explicit consent to store what you write. You can withdraw that consent at any time from your account. If you do, you can no longer add to wellbeing workbooks, and you can choose to delete what you have written.

Your work is protected in a way that goes beyond normal storage. In the words already shown in the app:

"Your answers are encrypted when stored. Only Akana's secure server can unlock them, and only to show them to you."

In technical terms: every answer, check-in and daily check is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it reaches the database. The encryption keys are held server side and never stored with the data. Each sealed value is bound to your account, the site you used and the field you answered, so it cannot be moved or read in another context. Unsealing happens only in server code acting for you while you are signed in. There is no administrative tool that can unseal your work. No author, publisher, white-label partner or member of Akana staff can read what you write.

Lawful basis: your explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)) [to confirm with the lawyer]. We have prepared a data protection impact assessment for this processing [DPIA reference].

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you
- **export** your work as a file, from your account, at any time
- **correct** anything that is wrong
- **delete** your account and your data, from your account, with a 7-day undo
- **withdraw consent** for anything that relies on consent, from your account
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests

- **complain** to the Information Commissioner's Office

Export and deletion are built into your account so you do not need to ask us. For anything else, email [privacy email]. We will respond within one month.
