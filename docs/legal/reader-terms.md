# Reader terms

These terms cover your use of Akana as a reader. Akana is run by [company name], a company registered in [country of registration] under number [company number], with its registered office at [registered address]. In these terms "we", "us" and "Akana" mean that company. "You" means the person using the service.

Separate terms apply to authors, publishers and white-label partners. They are not covered here.

## 1. Who the service is for

Akana is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account, buy a workbook or take out membership. We ask you to confirm this when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and refund any money paid.

## 2. What a workbook is, and what it is not

An Akana workbook is a self-guided programme built from a published book, under licence from its author or publisher. You read a section, do the exercises and keep your answers. You work at your own pace.

A workbook is not a medical, psychological, legal, financial or professional service. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. It is not therapy, counselling or coaching. It does not give you legal, tax or investment advice, and nothing in a workbook is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any product. If you already work with a doctor, therapist or other professional, a workbook can sit alongside that. It does not replace it.

We do not promise any result. What you get from a workbook depends on you. Authors write about what has worked for them and for others. That is not a guarantee it will work for you.

Some workbooks carry a short note at the start about who they are for and who they are not for. Please read it.

## 3. Your account

You need an account to use a workbook. You sign in with a link sent to your email address or with a sign-in provider we support. Keep your email account secure. You are responsible for what happens under your account unless we have failed in our own security duties.

Tell us straight away at [support email] if you think someone else has used your account.

## 4. Free first unit, purchases and immediate access

Most workbooks let you read and complete the first unit free once you have an account. The author may choose not to offer this.

To go further you buy the workbook or take out membership. Prices are shown in your local currency where we support it, including any VAT or sales tax due. [Company name] is the seller for every purchase on the Akana marketplace [to confirm: seller of record decision].

When you buy a workbook you choose to start right away. Before you pay, we ask you to confirm this in words like these:

"I want access to start right now. I understand that once access starts, I lose my 14-day right to cancel and get a refund."

## 5. Membership

Membership gives you access to every workbook in the membership library while your membership is active. It is billed monthly or annually, in advance, and renews automatically until you cancel. We tell you the price and the renewal terms before you pay, and we ask you to agree to renewal in words like these:

"I agree that my pass renews automatically at the price above until I cancel."

We email you before an annual renewal. You can cancel at any time from your account. Cancelling is as easy as signing up.

If you cancel within 14 days of your membership starting, it ends at once and we refund the unused time on a pro rata basis, as the refund policy describes. If you cancel later, your membership stops renewing and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

When your membership lapses, the workbooks you reached through it become read only. You keep your own answers and can still read and export them. You cannot add new answers until you renew or buy the workbook.

Some workbooks may sit outside membership at the author's choice. The workbook page tells you.

## 6. Demo content

Some listings are marked "Demo". They are example workbooks written to show what Akana does. Their authors and imprints are invented. Demo items cannot be bought. If a demo item ever appears at checkout, the purchase will be refused.

## 7. Acceptable use

You may use Akana for your own personal, non-commercial learning. You may not:

- copy, scrape, record or redistribute workbook content beyond what the service lets you export
- share your account or your sign-in links with anyone else
- try to get round access controls, rate limits or the sealing of answers
- use the service to harm, harass or deceive anyone
- upload or enter anything unlawful

Workbook content belongs to its author or publisher and is licensed to us. Nothing here gives you any rights in it beyond reading and using it through the service.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these rules. Where we do, we will tell you why unless the law prevents it.

## 8. Your own work

Everything you type into a workbook is yours. We call it your work: your answers, check-ins, daily checks and plans.

Your work is sealed before it is stored and only you can unseal it, through your own signed-in session. No author, publisher, partner site or member of Akana staff can read it. The privacy notice explains this in more detail.

You can export your work at any time from your account, as a file and as a page you can print. Export covers your prompts and answers, not the author's teaching text.

If a workbook is withdrawn from Akana, or an author's licence ends, you keep access to the version you bought and to your own work [to confirm with the lawyer: question D9]. If a workbook is removed after a valid copyright complaint, the content goes, you keep your own work and your export, and we refund you.

## 9. Help now and emergencies

Akana is not an emergency service and we do not monitor what you write. If you feel unsafe or in crisis, use "Help now" in the app. It lists who to contact where you live. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

## 10. Availability and changes to the service

We aim to keep Akana available at all times but cannot promise it. We may take the service down for maintenance or to fix a problem. We may change or remove features. If a change takes away something you have paid for, we will tell you and refund you where the law or the refund policy requires it.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law does not let us limit.

We are responsible for loss you suffer that is a foreseeable result of our breaking these terms or failing to use reasonable care and skill. We are not responsible for loss that was not foreseeable, for loss caused by your own breach of these terms, or for any business loss. Workbooks are for personal use.

Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by these terms.
