# Refund policy

This policy applies to readers who buy a workbook or take out membership on the Akana marketplace. It is part of the reader terms. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by anything here.

## 1. Your right to cancel

If you live in the UK, the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you 14 days from the day you buy digital content to change your mind and get a full refund, unless you asked for access to start straight away and agreed that this ends your right to cancel.

On Akana you choose to start right away. Before you pay for a single workbook we ask you to confirm:

"I want access to start right now. I understand that once access starts, I lose my 14-day right to cancel and get a refund."

Before you pay for membership we ask you to confirm:

"I want access to start right now. I understand that if I cancel within 14 days, I will get a refund minus a proportionate amount for the days I have had access."

We record which wording you agreed to, and when.

## 2. What Akana promises beyond the law

**Membership.** If you cancel within 14 days of your membership starting, it ends at once and we refund the unused time pro rata: the amount you paid, multiplied by the unused whole days, divided by the days in the period, rounded down to the smallest unit of your currency. The 14-day window is a setting we may lengthen but not shorten. If you cancel after 14 days, your membership stops renewing and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. No refund is due for that period.

In the case of a withdrawal we refund you and you keep your own answers and your export.

**Something went wrong at checkout.** If you were charged but did not get access, tell us and we will fix the access or refund you.

## 3. How to ask

Membership cancellation is self-service. Go to your account, choose your membership and cancel. Two taps, no questions. If you are within 14 days the refund is issued automatically.

For anything else, email [support email] from the email address on your account. Tell us which purchase it is about and what went wrong. We aim to reply within [support response time] and to decide on a refund within [refund decision time].

## 4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Once we issue a refund, your bank or card provider usually shows it within [refund settlement time]. We cannot speed up your bank.

If we refund a purchase in full, access to that workbook ends. Your own answers stay in your account and you can still export them.

## 5. Demo items

Listings marked "Demo" are examples and cannot be bought. Checkout refuses them. If you believe you were charged for a demo item, contact us and we will refund you in full.

## 6. Authors and publishers

Authors and publishers are paid a share of net receipts: what we actually receive after tax, payment fees and refunds. When we refund a purchase, the refund is reversed on the author's or publisher's statement. If their statement for that period has already closed, the amount is taken from their next statement [to confirm: question D6]. This does not affect your right to a refund.

## 7. Disputes and chargebacks

If you ask your bank to reverse a payment instead of asking us, we may suspend access to the workbook while the dispute is open. We would rather you talked to us first. We will always try to sort it out.

## 8. Changes

We may change this policy. Changes do not affect purchases made before the change.
