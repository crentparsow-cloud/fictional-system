# Akana for organisations: terms

These terms apply when a business, charity, church or community group (the **Organisation**) buys access to Akana for its people. Akana is run by [company name], a company registered in [country of registration] under number [company number], with its registered office at [registered address] (**Akana**, **we**, **us**).

The contract is made up of the order form we agree with you (the **Order Form**), these terms and the data processing agreement in docs/legal/organisation-dpa.md (the **DPA**). If they conflict, the Order Form wins, then the DPA, then these terms.

## 1. Words we use

- **Licence**: the access set out in the Order Form. It has a number of Seats, a start date, an end date and a list of titles or the membership catalogue.
- **Seat**: a place for one person. A Seat is held by one Member at a time.
- **Member**: a person who takes a Seat by accepting an invitation from the Organisation.
- **Administrator**: a person the Organisation names to run its account on Akana.
- **Roster**: the email addresses the Organisation invites, which Seats are taken, and when.
- **Member Work**: everything a Member writes or records in Akana, including answers, check-ins, daily checks, plans, progress and consents.

## 2. What we provide

We give the Organisation an account on Akana with a console where its Administrators can invite people by email, see which Seats are taken, cancel invitations and release Seats. The console also shows, as counts, how many Members have started. Counts below the threshold we set (currently five) are not shown.

Each Member uses their own Akana account under the Akana reader terms. A Member may use an account they already have.

Groups, group schedules, facilitator guides, reports beyond the counts above, single sign-on and custom branding are not part of this service. We will tell you if that changes.

## 3. The privacy line

The Organisation will never see Member Work. We will not give it to you, whatever the reason, and no part of Akana can show it to you. This includes Administrators, managers, group leaders, the author and our own staff.

You agree:

- not to ask, press or reward a Member to show you their Member Work, or to tell you how far they have got;
- not to make the use of a wellbeing workbook, or any workbook on faith or belief, a condition of work, membership, volunteering or anything else [check legal: employment and equality law];
- to tell people that taking part is their choice, and that they can say no or stop at any time;
- not to use the counts in the console to try to find out about any one person.

## 4. Adults only

Akana is for people aged 18 or over. You must not invite anyone under 18. Each Member confirms they are 18 or over before they take a Seat. If we learn that a Member is under 18, we will release the Seat and close or restrict the account under the reader terms.

## 5. Invitations and Seats

- You may invite only people you have a lawful reason to contact, at an address you hold for them.
- An invitation holds a place on the Licence until it is accepted, cancelled or expires. You cannot send more invitations than you have free places.
- One person holds at most one Seat on a Licence.
- If a person says no to an invitation, you will not be able to invite that address again.
- We limit how many invitations can be sent in a day, and how often one address can be invited, to prevent misuse.
- When you release a Seat, or a Member leaves, the Seat becomes free for someone else. The person keeps their account and their Member Work, which becomes read only for titles they had through the Licence.

## 6. Fees, invoices and tax

The fees, the number of Seats and the titles are set out in the Order Form. We invoice the Organisation as the Order Form says. Unless it says otherwise, invoices are payable within 30 days, and fees are shown and charged with VAT where VAT applies [check accountant: overseas organisations, O8].

Seats can be added during a Licence; the Order Form sets how added Seats are charged. Seats are reduced only at renewal.

A pilot Licence may be free if the Order Form says so. A free pilot is in return for the feedback set out in the Order Form.

## 7. Term, renewal and ending

The Licence runs from the start date to the end date in the Order Form. It renews only if the Order Form says so, in which case either side may stop renewal by giving 30 days' written notice before the end date [check legal].

Either side may end the contract by written notice if the other materially breaks it and does not put it right within 30 days of being asked.

We may suspend the Organisation's access, without ending the contract, if an invoice is unpaid 14 days after we remind you, if you break section 3 or 4, or if we need to protect Members or the service. While suspended, Members cannot open titles through the Licence. Their Member Work is unaffected.

When the Licence ends, every Seat is released. Members keep their accounts and their Member Work. They can export it or carry on with their own membership. We delete the Roster as set out in the DPA.

## 8. Content and the licence to use it

Workbooks are made under licence from their authors and publishers. During the Licence, Members may use the titles it covers for their own learning and for discussion within the Organisation. The Organisation may not copy, share or publish workbook content, or run Akana content outside Akana, without our written agreement [check legal: the author licence must grant organisational use before any organisation goes live].

Titles can change. If a title covered by the Licence is withdrawn, we will tell you and, where we can, suggest another.

## 9. Help now and safeguarding

Akana is not a crisis or emergency service and does not monitor what Members write. Every Member can reach "Help now" from any screen.

The Organisation remains responsible for its own safeguarding duties, including any checks its own policy requires for people who lead groups. A church should tell Members how to reach its safeguarding lead. Akana has no direct messages and does not show one Member's contact details to another.

## 10. Data protection

For the Roster and the counts, the Organisation is the controller and Akana is its processor under the DPA. For each Member's own account and Member Work, Akana is an independent controller under its privacy notice and the reader terms, and the Organisation has no say over that data and no access to it [check legal].

The Organisation must give its people a privacy notice that names Akana as its processor for the Roster.

## 11. Our responsibility

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law does not let us limit.

Otherwise, each side's total liability under the contract in any year is limited to the fees paid or payable for that year [check legal: a floor for free pilots], and neither side is liable for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, or for indirect loss.

## 12. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms by giving the Organisation at least 15 days' notice by email [check legal: P2B notice periods]. A change applies from the next renewal unless the law requires it sooner or it is needed to protect Members. Which version the Organisation accepted, and when, is recorded.

## 13. General

The Organisation may not transfer the contract without our written agreement. Nothing in the contract creates a partnership or agency. If a part of these terms is found unenforceable, the rest still applies. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction [check legal: Scottish and Northern Irish organisations].

## Notes for the lawyer (not part of the terms)

- The controller and processor split in section 10 follows akana-business.md section 3.1. The aim is that "never visible to your employer" holds in law as well as in code: if the Organisation were controller of Member Work, it could instruct Akana to disclose it.
- Section 3's bar on making wellbeing titles compulsory follows the ICO's view that consent must be freely given, and the power imbalance at work.
- A church's Roster can reveal religious belief. The church relies on Article 9(2)(d) for its own roster; Akana cannot. The faith consent screen (F-150) covers Akana's own processing for faith titles.
- The terms register (public.terms_versions, F-122) has no document type for these terms yet. One will be added with the approved text; acceptance is recorded per Organisation.
- No prices appear here on purpose. Prices are proposals until Crent sets them (N2).
