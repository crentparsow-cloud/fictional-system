# Cookie statement

This statement covers every site run on the Akana platform, including the Akana marketplace and any publisher's branded site on it.

## What we use

We use one kind of cookie: the cookie that keeps you signed in.

| Name | Purpose | Set when | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-akana-auth` | Holds your sign-in session so you stay signed in as you move between pages | When you sign in | [session length, to confirm from the auth configuration] |

The `__Host-` prefix means the cookie is sent only over a secure connection, only to the site that set it, and cannot be read by any other site or subdomain. It is marked HttpOnly, so scripts in the page cannot read it.

This cookie is strictly necessary to provide the service you have asked for. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations we do not need your consent to set it, so there is no cookie banner.

## What we do not use

- No analytics cookies.
- No advertising or marketing cookies.
- No third-party scripts, pixels or tags on any page of any site on the platform. Our content security policy blocks them by construction.
- No cross-site tracking.

Publisher sites on Akana use the same platform and the same rules. A publisher cannot add tracking to its site on Akana.

The platform reads the website address you visit to decide which site to show you. This is done on the server and does not use a cookie.

## Local storage

Your browser has a small store that belongs to this site only. We use it for things that stay on your device and are not sent to us as tracking:

- drafts of answers you are typing, so nothing is lost if your connection drops before a save
- your display preferences, such as text size, theme and reduced motion
- the pages you have already loaded, so the workbook can open offline

None of this is used to identify or track you. Saved answers are sent to our server only when you are signed in and only to your own account, where they are sealed as the privacy notice describes.

## How to clear cookies and local storage

Signing out removes the sign-in cookie. To clear everything this site has stored on your device, use your browser's settings. Look for "Clear browsing data", "Site data" or "Cookies and site data", and choose this site. Clearing local storage removes your display preferences and any unsaved draft. Your saved work stays in your account.

## Changes

If we ever add a cookie or a store that needs your consent, we will update this statement and ask you first.
