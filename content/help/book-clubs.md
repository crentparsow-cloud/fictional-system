# Running a book club

How a group can work through a workbook together, and what the group can and cannot see.

## Finding a programme for a group

The [Book Clubs page](/book-clubs) lists programmes that suit a group. Each has its own page with a plan, meeting by meeting, with ground rules and four questions you can use at any meeting. Programmes about wellbeing are not listed there. A group is not the right place for them.

## Starting

The first unit of every programme is free. Ask each member to open it before your first meeting. Each person uses their own account, so everyone works at their own pace.

## What stays private

What each person writes in a workbook is private to them. Nobody in the group, including whoever leads it, can read it. The plan asks members to share what they notice and what they will do next. It never asks anyone to read out their answers.

## A group that shares a licence

If a church, a charity or a team wants to pay once for its members, see [Akana for organisations](/organisations) and the help page on [groups](/help/groups).

## Telling your minister

If you would like your church to use Akana, the [Tell your minister](/tell-your-minister) page has a short note you can send yourself.
