# The weekly newsletter

One short email a week with one exercise. This page covers signing up, confirming and stopping it.

## Signing up

Go to the [newsletter page](/newsletter) and give your email address. We send one message to check the address is yours. Until you press the button in that message, you are not on the list and nothing else is sent.

If the confirmation does not arrive in a few minutes, look in your junk folder. You can sign up again after ten minutes and we will send a fresh one. A link that has been used, or an address that was never confirmed, simply stops working. Addresses that are not confirmed within 14 days are deleted.

## What each issue holds

One exercise you can try in a few minutes, and a short note from the team. Sometimes there is a note from another author. It is always marked Sponsored, because we are paid to include it. No issue names a workbook, and none needs an account.

## Stopping it

Every issue has an Unsubscribe link at the bottom. Open it and press the button. It works straight away. Many email apps also show an Unsubscribe option of their own at the top of the message, and that works too.

## Your address

We use your address for the newsletter and nothing else. There are no tracking pixels in the emails. Read the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy) for how long we keep what we hold.
