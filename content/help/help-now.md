# Help now

Akana is not a crisis service. Nobody watches what you write, and we cannot respond to an emergency.

**If you are in danger now, or someone else is, call your local emergency number.**

## Where to find it

Every screen of a wellbeing workbook has a Help now button. It opens the [Help now page](/help-now), which lists free crisis and support lines where you live. You do not need to sign in to use it.

The page shows lines for the UK, Ireland, the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. Each number is checked against official sources, and the page shows the date it was last checked. If you live somewhere else, the page links to a helpline finder for your country.

## What it is not

Akana workbooks are for wellness and learning. They are not treatment, therapy or medical advice. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor or another health professional.
