# Appearance settings

You can change how your Akana pages look once you are signed in: Home, Today, Toolkit, Library, You and the workbooks you open. The settings are in You, under Appearance.

## What you can change

- **Text size.** Four steps, from Standard to Largest.
- **Reading font.** Atkinson or Lexend.
- **Dim after 9 p.m.** Softer colours late in the evening, from 9 p.m. until 5 a.m. by your device's clock.
- **Reduce motion.** Screens change without movement. Your phone's or computer's own reduced motion setting is always followed as well, whether or not you tick this.

Each change applies straight away.

## Where they are saved

Appearance settings are saved on this device only, in this browser. They are not saved to your account, so a new phone, another browser or a cleared browser starts with the standard look.

If your browser is set not to keep site data, the settings apply to the page you are on and are lost when you leave it. The settings page tells you when this happens.
