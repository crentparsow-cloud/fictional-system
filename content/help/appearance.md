# Appearance and reading settings

You can change how Akana looks and reads once you are signed in. The Aa button on any workbook screen opens the reading settings. The same settings are in You, under Appearance.

## What you can change

- **Font.** Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend, a dyslexia-friendly face based on OpenDyslexic, or your device's own serif.
- **Text size.** Six steps, from Standard to Largest.
- **Page colour.** Match your device, Light or Dark.
- **Margins.** Narrow, Standard or Wide. Wider margins give a narrower column of text, which many people find easier to follow.
- **Progress.** Show the dots and the time estimate, the time estimate only, or neither.
- **Dim after 9 p.m.** Softer colours late in the evening, from 9 p.m. until 5 a.m. by your device's clock.
- **Reduce motion.** Screens change without movement. Your phone's or computer's own reduced motion setting is always followed as well, whether or not you tick this.

Each change applies straight away.

## Where they are saved

Font, text size, page colour, margins and progress are saved to your account, so they follow you to your phone, your laptop and any other browser you sign in on. They are also kept on the device you are using, so they still apply with no connection. If the two ever differ, the one you changed most recently is used.

Dim after 9 p.m. and Reduce motion are saved on the device only.

If your browser is set not to keep site data, your account copy still applies when you sign in. On a device that cannot keep anything, the settings apply to the page you are on and are lost when you leave it.

## A note on the dyslexia-friendly font

The dyslexia-friendly option is a trimmed copy of OpenDyslexic, which is free to use under the SIL Open Font Licence. Research on whether any font helps people with dyslexia is mixed, so it is there as a choice, not a promise. If another option reads more easily for you, use that.
