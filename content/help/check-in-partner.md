# Check-in partner

A check-in partner is someone you trust who gets a short update when you reach a new stage in a workbook. It is optional and you can stop at any time.

## What your partner sees

You choose how much they get:

1. That you reached a new stage, given only as a number, such as "stage 2 of 4".
2. As above, plus a gentle question they could ask you.
3. As above, plus a short note you write for them.

Your partner never sees your answers, the workbook's title, its Theme, the names of its stages or anything you wrote in it. Progress in wellbeing workbooks is shared only if you tick the box to include them. It is off unless you do.

## Inviting someone

In You, under Check-in partner, enter their first name and email address. They get one email asking if they would like updates. They do not need an account.

- You can have one partner at a time.
- You can send three invitations a month.
- If someone says no, we will not ask them again.

## Updates

Updates go out at most once a week, and at most four times in 30 days. Each stage is sent once. Your partner can reply with a short message, which you see in You.

## Stopping

You can stop sharing at any time in You. Your partner can also stop updates from any email. Each link in those emails does one thing only and expires.
