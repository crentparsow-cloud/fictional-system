# Groups

A group follows one workbook together, run through your organisation. This page is for members and for group leaders.

## Joining a group

Groups are part of a place from your organisation. See [organisations and seats](/help/organisations). If your organisation runs a group you can join, Home and Today show a line that says so, and the group appears on the Groups page. Nobody is added to a group without pressing a button. Joining is your choice.

A group run by a church, or on a faith workbook, asks for your faith consent before you join, because joining can show your religious beliefs. You can withdraw it in You.

## What the group can see

What you write stays private. Nobody in the group, its leader or your organisation can read it. Other members cannot see your details, and you cannot message each other on Akana. Your organisation sees how many have joined once there are at least 5, never who.

## The schedule

The leader sets the date each unit opens. Home, Today and the Groups page show which week your group is on. Nothing is locked by the schedule. You can read ahead or catch up at your own pace.

On the Groups page you can download the unit dates as a calendar file. You choose what the entries say: "Group: unit 1 opens", the workbook's name, or your own words. Anyone who sees your calendar will see those words.

## Check-ins

Each week you can answer "How was this week?" with one of three choices: Doing fine, Found it hard or Missed this week. It is optional, and there is no space to write anything.

You choose whether your check-ins count in the group's totals. In groups run by a business this is off unless you turn it on. In other groups it starts on, and you can turn it off. If your check-ins do not count, they are kept for you only. The leader sees how many chose each answer, never who, and nothing until at least 5 have counted.

Under Your choices for this group you can change whether you count, delete your check-ins for the group, or leave the group. Leaving keeps your seat, your workbook and everything you wrote.

## Reporting a concern

Every group has a link to report a concern. The report goes to the Akana support team, never to the leader or your organisation. If someone is in danger now, call 999 in the UK or your local emergency number. Akana is not a crisis service and cannot check on anyone. [Help now](/help-now) lists free support lines.

For a church group, please also speak to your church's safeguarding lead. Akana is not a safeguarding service.

## If you lead a group

Your organisation's owner asks you to lead or co-lead. The request shows on the Groups page, and you accept or say no there.

The leader view shows the group's week, the schedule, how many have joined, and how many chose each check-in answer for a unit. It never shows who chose what, anyone's answers or anyone's contact details. Only check-ins people chose to count are included, up to the end of yesterday, and a number under 5 is not shown.

If there is a facilitator guide for the workbook, the leader view shows the part for the current unit, and you can open the whole guide to print. Please keep it among the group's leaders.

You can change the unit dates while the group is running or not yet started. Leave a date empty to keep that unit off the schedule.

Please do not ask people to read out or show what they wrote. Taking part, and checking in, is always each person's choice. In a church group, if someone tells you something that worries you about their safety or someone else's, follow your church's safeguarding policy.
