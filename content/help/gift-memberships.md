# Gift memberships

You can give someone a membership for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. You pay once. It does not renew.

## How it works

1. Go to the Give a membership page, from You or from the [pricing page](/pricing).
2. Choose how long the gift lasts and type their email address. Add your name and a short message if you like.
3. Pay once on Stripe's secure page.
4. We email them a link. We do not name any workbook in the email.
5. They sign in or make an account, press Claim my gift, and the whole membership library opens.

They do not need a card. Nothing is taken from them, now or later.

## What they get

The same library as a paying member, for the length you chose. Their answers stay private. You cannot read them and neither can we.

## When the gift ends

Nothing renews and nothing is charged. Their account goes back to how it was. Everything they wrote stays theirs and stays readable. They can join the membership any time if they want to carry on.

## If they do not claim it

A gift can be claimed for a year after you buy it. The Give a membership page shows your gifts, whether each has been claimed, and the link, so you can pass it on yourself if the email goes astray. After a year an unclaimed gift ends and we delete the address you gave us.

## Refunds

If you ask for a refund of a gift that has not been claimed, the gift ends and the link stops working. See [refunds](/help/refunds).
