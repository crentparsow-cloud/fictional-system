# Membership

What membership includes, how it renews and how to manage it.

## What it includes

Membership opens every workbook marked as included in membership on the Akana marketplace, for as long as your membership is active. Demo workbooks are examples. They are never part of membership and cannot be bought. Some workbooks may be sold on their own and not be included.

Current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## How it renews

You choose monthly or yearly when you join. Membership renews automatically until you cancel. The payment page says so above the pay button.

We remind you by email:

- if you pay yearly, before each renewal
- if you pay monthly, every six months, with the price, how often you pay, your next payment date and how to cancel

## Managing your membership

In You, under Membership, choose Manage membership. This opens Stripe's secure page, where you can:

- cancel
- change your card
- switch between monthly and yearly
- see and download your invoices

Akana never sees your full card number.

## If a payment fails

Your card provider may decline a renewal. We keep your membership open for 14 days while the payment is tried again. If it still fails, the membership ends. Everything you wrote stays in your account.

## When membership ends

Your answers, check-ins and plans stay in your account. You can still download them. To carry on with a workbook, you can join again or buy that workbook on its own if it is sold that way.

See [cancelling](/help/cancelling) and [refunds](/help/refunds) for what happens to your money.
