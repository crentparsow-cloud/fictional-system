# Add Akana to your home screen

How to put Akana on your phone like an app, and why it helps if you write when you are offline.

## On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open Akana in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
4. Open Akana from your home screen from now on.

## On an Android phone

Open Akana in Chrome. When we ask whether you would like it on your home screen, say yes and follow the browser's prompt. You can also open the browser menu and choose Install app.

## Why it helps

Safari can clear what a website keeps on your device if you have not visited for seven days. That could include answers you have written but not yet saved to your account. A web app on your home screen is not cleared this way and keeps its own clock, so it is the safer place for unsaved work. Anything saved to your account is never affected.

On a computer, Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

## Signing in from the app

A sign-in link in an email opens your browser, not the app. The email also has a six-digit code. Open the app, ask for a sign-in email on the sign-in page, and type the code in. You can also choose Continue with Google.

Your bookmark to Today keeps working, with or without the app.

See [your account](/help/account) for more about signing in.
