# Student price

Students get half off the membership. We check with an academic email address.

## How to get it

1. Go to You and choose Student price. It only appears when the student price is switched on.
2. Type your academic email address. It must end the way a university or college address ends, for example .ac.uk or .edu.
3. We email you a link. It stops working after a short time. Open it and press Confirm.
4. Choose monthly or yearly and pay. The student price is shown beside the standard price, so you can see both.

## What we keep

We keep a scrambled copy of your address and the ending of it, not the address itself. The link we send is used once.

## How long it lasts

Your confirmation lasts a year. About a month before it ends we email you a reminder to confirm again. If you confirm, the student price carries on. If you do not, the discount comes off your membership on the end date, and your membership goes on at the standard price until you cancel. We email you when that happens.

## Good to know

- The student price is for a new membership. It cannot be added to one you already have.
- Cancelling and pausing work the same as for any membership. See [cancelling membership](/help/cancelling).
- Prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
