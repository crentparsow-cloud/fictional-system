# Cancelling membership

You can cancel at any time yourself. You do not need to contact us or give a reason.

## How to cancel

1. Go to You.
2. Under Membership, choose Manage membership.
3. On Stripe's secure page, choose Cancel.

## Within 14 days

If you cancel within 14 days of your membership starting, or within 14 days of a yearly renewal, your membership ends straight away and we refund the unused days automatically. The refund is the amount you paid, multiplied by the unused whole days, divided by the days in the period, rounded down to the smallest unit of your currency. We email you to confirm the amount.

## After 14 days

If you cancel later, your membership stops renewing. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. No refund is due for that period. We email you to confirm the date it ends.

## Your work

Cancelling never deletes anything you wrote. To delete your work as well, see [your account](/help/account).
