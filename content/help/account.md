# Your account

How signing in works, what we ask when you join, and how to download or delete your work.

## Signing in

There is no password. Enter your email address on the sign-in page and we send you a link. Open it in the same browser you asked from. The link works once and expires after a short time. If it has expired, ask for a new one.

## When you join

We ask you to confirm two things before you start:

- that you are 18 or over, because Akana workbooks are written for adults
- that you agree to the [reader terms](/legal/terms), which include the [refund policy](/legal/refunds) and point to the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy)

We record which version of the terms you agreed to, and when. If we change the terms, we ask you to agree again the next time you open your account or pay for something. Nothing you have written is affected.

## Wellbeing workbooks

Before you open your first wellbeing workbook, we ask for your explicit consent to store what you write, because it may be health information. You can withdraw that consent at any time in You, under Your data. If you do, you can no longer add to wellbeing workbooks. What you have already written stays until you delete it.

## Download your work

In You, under Download my work, you can download:

- a data file (JSON) with every answer, check-in and plan
- a printable page you can print or save as a PDF from your browser

The printable page leaves the book title out unless you tick the box to show it.

## Delete your account

In You, under Your data, choose Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm. Your account and everything you have written are deleted 7 days later. Until then you can change your mind and cancel the deletion from the same page.

Purchase records are kept after deletion because tax law requires it. They do not include your work. If you have a membership, deleting your account also ends it. If you are within 14 days of starting it or of a yearly renewal, the unused days are refunded.
