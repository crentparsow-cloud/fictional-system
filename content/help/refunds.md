# Refunds

The full rules are in the [refund policy](/legal/refunds). This page is a short guide. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by anything here.

## Membership

If you cancel within 14 days of starting, or of a yearly renewal, the unused days are refunded automatically. See [cancelling](/help/cancelling).

## Single workbooks

A workbook opens as soon as you pay. The refund policy explains how that affects your 14-day right to cancel.

Whether we also offer a goodwill refund window for single workbooks has not been decided yet. The refund policy will say when it is.

## Something went wrong

If a workbook does not work, is not what its page described, or is withdrawn, you are entitled to a repair, a replacement or a refund. If you were charged but did not get access, tell us and we will fix the access or refund you.

## Demo workbooks

Demo workbooks cannot be bought. Checkout refuses them. If you believe you were charged for one, contact us and we will refund you in full.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you used. Your bank decides how long it takes to show.

If a workbook is refunded in full, access to it ends. Your answers stay in your account and you can still download them.

## Asking for a refund

Email us from the address on your account. Tell us which purchase it is about and what went wrong. Our address is at the bottom of this page.
