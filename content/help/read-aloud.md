# Reading aloud

Akana can read a workbook screen to you using your device's own voice. It is useful when your eyes are tired, when you are cooking or walking, or when you simply take things in better by ear.

## How to use it

On a workbook screen, choose Listen at the top. A bar appears above the tabs with Play, Back and Next, a speed control and the sentence being read. The sentence is highlighted on the page as it is spoken. Choose Read to stop and go back to plain reading.

If you pause, or leave and come back, it starts again from the sentence you stopped on.

## What it reads, and what it does not

It reads the writing in the workbook: introductions, key ideas, steps, examples and closing lines. It never reads your answers aloud. What you have written is not part of what it reads, so it is safe to use with other people in the room.

It does not read the questions in a form while you are filling it in, or the buttons and menus.

## What the voice cannot do

The voice belongs to your device or browser, not to Akana, so it varies from one device to the next. Please know its limits.

- It will not sound like a person. It may stress the wrong word, read a name oddly or run two ideas together.
- It cannot read in a different mood. A hard passage will sound as flat as an easy one.
- It may not be able to say numbers, dates, abbreviations or words from another language correctly.
- Some devices have only one voice, or none in the language of the workbook. If there is no matching voice, the Listen switch tells you and nothing is read in the wrong language.
- On some browsers the voice stops when you lock your phone or switch to another app. Start it again and it carries on from the same sentence.
- The speed goes up to twice normal. Some voices sound poor when sped up.
- It is not a recording. It cannot be downloaded, and it does not work for a screen that is mostly a form.

Nothing is sent anywhere to make the voice work. The words are spoken on your device.

## If it does not work

Check that your device is not on silent and that the volume is up. Try another browser. On an iPhone or iPad, Safari works best. If you still have no sound, tell us which device and browser you use through the contact details in your account page.
