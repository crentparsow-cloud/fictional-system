# Privacy

The full details are in the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy). This is what the product does today.

## Your answers

What you write in a workbook is encrypted before it is stored. Only Akana's secure server can unlock it, and only to show it to you. No author, publisher, white-label partner or member of Akana staff can read what you write. There is no screen or tool that shows your answers to anyone else.

## Emails

Our emails never name the workbook you are using. Someone who sees your inbox cannot tell what you are working on.

## Cookies and tracking

There are no ad pixels and no third-party trackers on Akana. We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and the site secure, so there is no cookie banner. See the [cookie statement](/legal/cookies).

## Health information

What you write in a wellbeing workbook may be health information. We ask for your explicit consent before we store it, and you can withdraw it at any time. See [your account](/help/account).

## Check-in partner

If you invite a check-in partner, they never see your answers, the workbook, its Theme or anything you wrote in it. See [check-in partner](/help/check-in-partner).

## Your rights

You can download your work and delete your account yourself in You. To ask for a copy of anything else we hold about you, contact us. We respond within one month.
