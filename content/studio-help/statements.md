# Statements and earnings

The Earnings page in the Studio shows what your workbooks have earned, what is waiting to be paid and your monthly statements. Owners, finance and authors in your organisation can see it. It never shows who bought or read a workbook.

## How your share is worked out

For each sale we start with what the reader paid. We take off VAT, and the card fee where your licence says so. What is left is the net receipt. Your share is the net receipt times the rate in your licence.

If a reader gets a refund, your share of that sale comes off the next statement. Membership money is shared each month across the workbooks members used, by how many steps they completed, up to a cap. See [the membership pool](/studio/help/pool).

The rates are not set yet. While they are placeholders, figures are marked Provisional, and nothing is paid out at those rates.

## Balance

For each currency the page shows three amounts: earned but not yet paid, ready to pay, and a payout on its way. It also shows the last month that closed. Payouts go to the bank account on your Payouts page. See [how payment works](/studio/help/payments).

## Statements

A month closes 14 days after it ends, so late refunds can land in it. Then it gets a statement. Each statement shows sales, refunds, membership, what was paid out and the closing balance. You can download each one as a PDF or a CSV file. Your first statement appears after your first month with sales closes.

## By workbook and month

Below the statements, a table shows each workbook for each month: copies sold, what readers paid, the rate, your share of sales, refunds, membership and your total share. Choose a range of months to see, and download the table as a CSV file.

Figures from Stripe test mode are marked Test.
