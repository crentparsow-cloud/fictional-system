# Demo accounts

Akana keeps a demo publisher for sales calls and walkthroughs. This page explains what a demo login shows and what it cannot do.

## The demo publisher

Quillmoor Demo Press is invented. It has two imprints, two invented authors and four workbooks, one in each state the Studio shows: live, paused, in review and draft. Everything is labelled Demo. It also has a demo site in its own branding.

There are two demo logins: a demo publisher, who owns Quillmoor Demo Press, and a demo author, who signs in as one of its authors. Akana staff create these sign-ins and register them on the admin demo page. A staff account, or an account that belongs to a real organisation, cannot be a demo login.

## What you see

After a reset, the demo logins see figures on Earnings, Dashboard and the publisher roll-up: about six months of test-mode sales, a few refunds, a statement for each month whose refund window has passed, and monthly dashboard counts. Some counts sit below the threshold of 10, so you can show how small numbers are hidden. Demo statements are marked DEMO and downloads carry "-demo" in the file name.

These are invented figures. No reader paid and no money moved. They are kept in separate demo tables and never reach a real statement or payout. Demo workbooks cannot be bought and are not in the membership.

## Resets

The demo is put back every night at 02:37 UTC, and staff can reset it at any time. A reset restores the names, imprints, authors, the four workbook states, the site's look and catalogue, the members and the demo money. Anything you change during a call is undone. Workbooks a demo login added are retired, and anyone a demo login invited is removed.
