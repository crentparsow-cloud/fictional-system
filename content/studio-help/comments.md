# The comment thread

Each workbook has one comment thread between your organisation and the Akana team. You find it on the workbook's page in the Studio, under Comments. Akana reviewers see the same thread in their review screen.

## Who can read and post

Everyone in your organisation who can see the workbook can read the thread. Owners, editors and authors can post. On the Akana side, staff owners, editors and safety reviewers read and post.

## Writing a comment

Comments are plain text, up to 2,000 characters, with no attachments. Line breaks are kept. Links are shown as text and are not made clickable. A comment cannot be edited or deleted once posted, so the thread stays a true record of what was said.

Each person can post up to 20 comments an hour.

## Emails about comments

When you post, the other side gets an email saying a comment is waiting, with a link to the workbook. The email never contains the comment itself. On our side it goes to the reviewers assigned to the workbook and anyone at Akana already on the thread, or to the review team's inbox if nobody is assigned yet. On your side it goes to your organisation's owners, editors and authors.

If the same side posts several comments within 10 minutes, only the first sends an email, so a run of comments does not fill anyone's inbox.
