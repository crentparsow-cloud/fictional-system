# Pricing

Akana sells every workbook at one of six fixed points on a price ladder. Point 1 is for the shortest workbooks and point 6 for the longest.

## Why a ladder

Readers see the same prices across the store, in their own currency. Each point has a set price in each currency, chosen by Akana, so there are no odd conversions. Free-text prices would break that, and would make the membership sums hard to follow.

## Choosing a point

On the workbook's page in the Studio, pick the point that fits its length. Akana approves it with the workbook, and may suggest a different point. If we do, we say why.

The figures for each point are not set yet. Until they are, the Studio shows "price to be confirmed". Nothing can be sold at a placeholder price.

## The membership

Akana members can open workbooks in the membership catalogue. Your licence says whether your book's workbooks can be in it. If it can, you choose whether to include it. If your licence does not allow it, the workbook is sold on its own.

## Your share

The Studio will show an estimate of your share of each sale, after VAT and payment fees. The share and the fee figures are not set yet, so the estimate is not shown. We will not show a number until it is real.

Demo workbooks are never sold and have no price.
