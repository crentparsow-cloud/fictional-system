# The membership pool

Akana members pay a monthly or yearly fee and can open any workbook in the membership catalogue. Part of that income will go to the authors whose workbooks members use. We call this the pool.

## What we propose

The pool is not running yet. Here is the proposal, so you know where it is going.

- A share of membership income goes into the pool each month.
- The pool is split by how much members use each workbook, counted in completed steps, with a cap so that no single reader counts too much.
- The count uses which steps were completed, never what a reader wrote. Reader answers are private and sealed.

## What is not decided

The share of membership income that goes into the pool, and the exact rule for splitting it, are not decided. They will be written into the licence before anyone signs. Until then, nothing here is a promise.
