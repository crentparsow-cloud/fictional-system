# How review works

From submission to live, a workbook passes through a few clear steps. You can see where yours is on its page in the Studio.

## The steps

1. **Submitted.** You send the book and your brief. We email you to confirm.
2. **Accepted.** An Akana editor takes it on. We email you when this happens.
3. **In review.** The editor builds or checks the workbook. Our automatic checks run on every version. If changes are needed, we send them to you with the reasons, by email and on the workbook's page.
4. **Your sign-off.** When it is ready, we ask you to preview it in the real reader and sign off.
5. **Other sign-offs.** An Akana editor signs off. Wellbeing workbooks also need a safety reviewer, and the most sensitive ones a named clinician. Faith workbooks need a theological reviewer.
6. **Approved.** Every sign-off is in. We email you. It is not on sale yet.
7. **Live.** Readers can find it. We email you on the day.

If a workbook is ever paused, we email you with the reason. Readers who already have it keep their access.

## Previewing

Preview opens the workbook in the same reader that readers use. You can switch between a phone and a desktop width, light and dark, and 200% text. You can type into the exercises to try them. Nothing you type is saved, and nothing is sold.

## Signing off an exact version

Each version of a workbook has a fingerprint of its content, a long code made from every word in it. You sign off that fingerprint. If anything changes after you sign, even one word, the fingerprint changes and we ask you to sign the new version. So the workbook readers get is always the one you approved.

When you sign, you also approve the title, the short title, the card line that shows in the Library, and the badge.

## How long it takes

We do not have a fixed timeline yet, because we are only now opening to the first authors. We will tell you at each step what happens next and roughly when.
