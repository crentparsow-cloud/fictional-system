# How payment works

Akana is the seller of every workbook. Readers pay Akana, and Akana pays you your share.

## Getting paid

Payouts go through Stripe. Set up your payout account on the Payouts page in the Studio. Stripe asks for the details it needs to pay you and to meet the law where you live. A workbook goes live only once its organisation's payouts are set up.

If your payout or tax details change, the owners and finance contacts in your organisation get an email straight away, as a security check.

## Statements

You will get a statement for each period, showing sales, refunds and your share. We email you when a new one is ready.

## What is not set yet

The payout day, the minimum payout and the payout currency are still being decided. We will tell you before the first payout.
